I'm someone who gets really inspired and moved by things. Usually these things are pieces of media, or other people. *Beautiful* is a heavy-lifting adjective for me. 

Some **movies** that have shaped me are [Whale Rider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whale_Rider), [Princess Mononoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Mononoke), [Ajami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajami_(film)), and [Avatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar_(2009_film)). I'm also a huge [Avatar: the Last Airbender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_The_Last_Airbender) fan. I've been less shaped by, but still moved by, [Across the Spiderverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Across_the_Spider-Verse), [Brand New Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Brand_New_Day), [Battlestar Galactica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battlestar_Galactica_(2004_TV_series)), and [Arcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcane_(TV_series)). I deeply love the short film [Two Cars, One Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_Cars,_One_Night). 

I have perhaps been even more shaped by **books**: the [Green Bone Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_City_(novel)), [Unsong](https://unsongbook.com/), [The Thief Lord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Thief_Lord), [The Sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sparrow_(novel)), and the entire [Ender Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ender%27s_Game_(novel_series)). My favorite book is [Xenocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenocide). I also love some good nonfiction, like [The Sequences](https://www.lesswrong.com/rationality), [The Gene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gene:_An_Intimate_History), and [Endless Forms Most Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endless_Forms_Most_Beautiful_(book)), and some short stories, like those in [Ken Liu's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Liu) and [Ted Chiang's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Chiang) books. Growing up, I was a huge fan of [Warrior Cats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warriors_(novel_series)), [Guardians of Ga'Hoole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardians_of_Ga%27Hoole), and [Wings of Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wings_of_Fire_(novel_series)) (and still am). 

My **music** taste has steadily gotten more contemporary over the years, but my favorite album is still [Aijuswanaseing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aijuswanaseing) by [Musiq Soulchild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musiq_Soulchild). I also love the [Octopath Traveler soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopath_Traveler), [Oneohtrix Point Never](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oneohtrix_Point_Never), [Nas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nas), the [Battlestar Galactica soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Battlestar_Galactica_(2004_TV_series)), the [Black Hawk Down soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Hawk_Down_(soundtrack)), the [Prince of Narnia soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia:_Prince_Caspian_(soundtrack)) (have never even seen these last two movies), and most recently [Laundry Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laundry_Day_(band)). My favorite artist is still probably [slchld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slchld), and my favorite composer has been [Ludwig Göransson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_G%C3%B6ransson) ever since [Black Panther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Panther_(soundtrack)).
